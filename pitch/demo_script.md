# PowerPool — Live Hackathon Demo Script (3 Minutes)

## 0:00 - 0:30: The Problem Statement & Hook
"Judges, residential solar is booming across India, but local distribution networks (DISCOMs) are struggling with a hidden problem: **renewable intermittency and evening transformer overloading**.

At 1:00 PM, neighbourhood rooftop solar produces surplus energy that goes unutilized. By 7:00 PM, families return home, turn on ACs, washing machines, and EV chargers—causing peak feeder demand to exceed transformer capacity by 15-20%, leading to power outages and transformer failures.

Introducing **PowerPool**: a neighbourhood-scale load-flexibility platform that forecasts feeder load, detects stress windows, and shifts flexible household loads into solar-rich hours."
