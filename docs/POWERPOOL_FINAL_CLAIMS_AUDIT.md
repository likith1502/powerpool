## 1. PURPOSE & CLASSIFICATION FRAMEWORK

This audit defines the authoritative, verified claims boundary for PowerPool presentations, demo narratives, and slide materials. Every claim is categorized by its verification status:
- **Independently Verified:** Re-evaluated, executed, or inspected directly during current engineering audits.
- **Supplied Audit Finding:** Established by the previous rigorous technical audit and cross-checked against project artifacts.
- **Simulated Model Output:** Derived from mathematical or algorithmic simulation within the software, clearly demarcated from real-world hardware measurement.
- **Unverified / Removed:** Statements that lack reproducible artifact backing or conflate distinct configurations.
